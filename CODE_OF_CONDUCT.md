# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors, maintainers, and community members, pledge to make participation in the **Enterprise AI Architecture in C++** project a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to fostering an open, collaborative, and respectful community where everyone can learn, share ideas, and contribute with confidence.

---

# Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and considerate of others.
* Using welcoming and inclusive language.
* Accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
* Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
* Focusing on what is best for the community.
* Demonstrating empathy toward other community members.
* Helping newcomers learn and grow.
* Providing thoughtful code reviews and technical discussions.
* Sharing knowledge openly and professionally.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment or discriminatory language of any kind.
* Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
* Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or hostile behavior.
* Publishing private information without explicit permission.
* Repeatedly disrupting discussions or community activities.
* Spam, abusive messages, or offensive content.
* Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

---

# Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

* Clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.
* Responding fairly and consistently to reports of unacceptable behavior.
* Taking appropriate corrective action when necessary.
* Maintaining a respectful and welcoming environment.
* Acting in the best interests of the project and its contributors.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including but not limited to:

* GitHub Issues
* Pull Requests
* Discussions
* Project documentation
* Source code contributions
* Community chat platforms
* Official project websites
* Conference presentations and community events representing the project

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

---

# Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the project maintainers.

When submitting a report, please include:

* A description of the incident.
* The date and time (if known).
* Any relevant links, screenshots, or supporting information.
* The usernames or individuals involved.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion and respect for everyone involved.

---

# Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may take actions they consider appropriate depending on the severity and frequency of the violation, including:

1. Private warning.
2. Public warning.
3. Temporary restriction from community participation.
4. Temporary suspension.
5. Permanent removal from the project community.

Enforcement decisions will be made with fairness, consistency, and respect for all participants.

# Community Values

We believe great software is built through collaboration.

Our community values:

* Respect
* Integrity
* Inclusivity
* Professionalism
* Transparency
* Innovation
* Knowledge sharing
* Continuous learning
* Technical excellence

Every contributor—whether submitting a typo fix, improving documentation, adding tests, reporting bugs, or implementing major features—plays an important role in the success of this project.
